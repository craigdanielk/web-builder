# THURIS media generation brief

Generation is allowed: **YES**.

## Non-negotiable visual system
- Brand: THURIS. Never render ORIVEL, AVELOR, VELD ATELIER or any other prior working name.
- Bottle target: 10 ml, compact square/rectangular heavy-bottom dark violet-black glass, concealed glass pipette/press-dropper mechanism, oversized brushed-champagne metal outer cap.
- Label: restrained THURIS wordmark; product name; botanical/source line only where specified in the build input. Do not invent certifications, ingredient claims, batch numbers, manufacturing addresses, awards, reviews or regulatory marks.
- Box target: rigid charcoal presentation box with restrained champagne-gold typography.
- Lighting: low-key studio lighting, warm rim/side light, deep controlled shadow, readable front label.
- Materials: dark stone, charcoal, smoked plum, warm parchment only.
- Composition: architectural negative space, museum-object treatment, no clutter.
- Camera: product-advertising realism, 50–100 mm equivalent, clean perspective, realistic glass/metal reflections.

## Cultural direction
The launch market is the UAE, but imagery must not resort to pseudo-Arabic ornament, mosque imagery, dunes/camels, incense-burner clichés or unapproved Arabic calligraphy. UAE relevance should come from fragrance-house sophistication, ritual, gifting, materiality and restraint.

## Prohibited visual claims
Do not show laboratory certificates, organic seals, ECOCERT/USDA/IFRA marks, medical props, dermatology imagery, healing/wellness symbols, review stars, press logos, “100% PURE”, “THERAPEUTIC GRADE”, or any text implying therapeutic benefit.

## Required outputs
1. Hero: 2400 px minimum width, three bottles or one hero bottle with the trio secondary; dark stone and champagne highlights; leave usable negative space for headline/CTA.
2. Clean Frankincense product shot.
3. Clean Neroli product shot.
4. Clean Rose Geranium product shot.
5. Clean Founding Trio product shot.
6. Clean cap/neck macro.
7. Clean bottle + presentation box.
8. Clean concealed-dropper detail.
9. Lifestyle dark-stone still life.
10. Lifestyle gifting/box composition.
11. Lifestyle trio composition.
12. OG social image 1200×630.

## Accuracy gate
The bottle/box imagery is a design render until the final supplier specification is approved. If final production geometry, finish or closure differs, regenerate before public launch.
