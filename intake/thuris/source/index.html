Before you reserve

What is THURIS?

A botanical fragrance house. Its first release is the Founding Collection: Frankincense, Neroli and Rose Geranium, each a 10 ml botanical essence.

What does the name mean?

THURIS comes from the Latin thūs, thūris — incense, frankincense.

Are these perfumes?

No. They are 10 ml botanical essences, not eau de parfum.

How many bottles are there?

The Founding Release is limited to 90 bottles — 30 of each botanical essence. The complete Founding Collection uses one of each.

Why pre-order?

The Founding Release is a single, limited first bottling. Reserving secures a bottle from it before dispatch.

Do you publish purity claims?

Not without batch documentation to support them. We will not make a claim we cannot show.
