RITUAL

Scent is not only worn. It is placed.

Across the Gulf, fragrance is part of preparation. It settles into fabric, fills a room before guests arrive, and stays in the memory of a house long after.

It is built in layers — space, cloth, the person — each with its own material and moment.

THURIS enters that culture with respect and as a newcomer. We bring three materials to a practice that was refined long before us.
