# Example audit report (artisan food / demo)

## Business
Example brand for pipeline and audit documentation. Artisan food / coffee style. Use for testing brief → preset matching and section generation. Replace with real project brief when running a real build.

## What They Need
A modern, high-converting homepage for their headless Shopify storefront built with Next.js and deployed to Vercel. The homepage must showcase their heritage brand while driving product discovery across their core categories: Kaffee (99 products across Bohnen, Gemahlen, Kapseln, Spezialitäten), Tee (41 products), Maschinen (44 products across Kaffeemaschinen, Filtermethoden, Kaffeemühlen), and Zubehör (57 products). The site serves three markets: Switzerland (CHF), Austria (EUR), and Germany (EUR).

## Key Requirements
- Hero section emphasizing 260+ years of heritage and Swiss artisanal quality
- Featured product collections showcasing their core coffee range (Bohnen, Spezialitäten)
- Category navigation driving users to Kaffee, Tee, Maschinen, Zubehör
- Brand story section highlighting small-batch drum roasting tradition
- Social proof / trust signals (260 years, Swiss quality, artisanal craft)
- Barista Academy promotion (courses, corporate events)
- Newsletter signup for coffee enthusiasts
- Multi-market awareness (CH/AT/DE) though homepage is German-language primary

## Target Audience
Swiss, Austrian, and German coffee enthusiasts ranging from casual home brewers to serious home baristas. Gift buyers seeking premium Swiss coffee products. Professional baristas and hospitality businesses looking for equipment and training. Price range spans CHF 3.90 (capsules) to CHF 5,495 (La Marzocco bundles), indicating both everyday consumers and premium equipment buyers.

## Brand Personality
Warm, artisanal, heritage-proud, and educational. The brand balances Swiss precision with handcrafted tradition. Tone is inviting and knowledgeable — they want customers to feel welcomed into a 260-year coffee tradition, not intimidated by it. They educate through their Barista Academy and detailed product descriptions about origins, roasting profiles, and tasting notes. The brand feels like a knowledgeable friend who happens to run the oldest coffee roastery in Switzerland.

## Specific Requests
- Color palette: warm stone tones (stone-50 backgrounds, stone-900/950 text) with amber-700/800 accents
- Typography: DM Serif Display (700 weight) for headings, DM Sans (400 weight) for body text
- Border radius: medium (rounded-lg / rounded-xl)
- Animation: moderate intensity — fade-up-stagger entrances, lift-shadow hover effects
- Whitespace: generous (py-24 section padding, gap-8 internal spacing)
- Photography style: warm-toned product shots, close-up bean/roasting imagery, cozy café atmosphere
- No stock photography — use product images from their existing catalog where possible
- German language throughout (Willkommen, Entdecken, Unsere Geschichte, etc.)
