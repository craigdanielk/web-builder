# Brief: Fireforge

## Business
Fireforge (Pty) Ltd — premium South African brand selling custom-made, high-end
**stainless steel braais, Argentinian-style parrillas, outdoor fireplaces and fire
features**, plus branded accessories (Forge Axe kindling splitter, tool sets).
Direct-to-consumer, premium positioning. Backed by parent fabricator Elemetal
Design (precision laser-cut 304/316 stainless, Randburg) — in-house custom
fabrication is the trust anchor. Primary market: affluent SA homeowners
(Gauteng + Western Cape); secondary B2B: architects, interior designers, luxury
builders, developers, lodges/restaurants.

## What They Need
A cinematic, conversion-optimised e-commerce storefront that sells high-ticket
(R35k–R200k+) bespoke fire equipment. Two motions on one site: an immersive,
editorial **brand experience** (to justify the premium and tell the craft story)
fused with **hard CRO** (clear product offers, spec confidence, quote capture for
bespoke, PayFast checkout for transactable items, lead capture for B2B).

## Visual Target (clone reference)
Emulate the aesthetic of **https://www.exoape.com/** — full-bleed cinematic media,
buttery smooth-scroll (Lenis), oversized editorial typography, vast negative space,
custom cursor, scroll-pinned reveals, route transitions, horizontal galleries.
Blend that gallery-grade feel with product-commerce structure.

## Brand
- Palette: matte black + brushed 304 stainless, ember-orange accent, warm fire glow
- Motif: F-honeycomb laser-cut detail; industrial-luxury, precision, heritage
- Tone: confident, crafted, understated premium — not loud retail

## Key Requirements
- Smooth-scroll + motion layer matching exoape (Lenis + GSAP/Framer)
- Cinematic home: brand film hero → product showcase → craft story → CRO band
- Shop + Product (PDP): hero, spec, raise/lower mechanism feature macro, lifestyle
  scene, price, **configure/quote or PayFast buy**
- Custom Build: B2B/bespoke lead funnel (architect/designer channel)
- Craft/About: Elemetal fabrication story (trust)
- CRO: sticky CTA, trust badges (304 stainless · lifetime · locally forged), social
  proof, high-ticket quote capture, lead → Supabase
- Catalog + leads in Supabase; PayFast payments
- Deploy to Vercel; domain fireforge.co.za (to be registered)

## Products (initial catalog)
- Fireforge T100 (flagship Argentinian braai, raise/lower grills, 3 drawers, honeycomb brasero)
- Forge Axe (kindling/wood splitter)
- Fireforge Custom Build (bespoke tier)
- Fireforge Tool Set (branded stainless tools)
