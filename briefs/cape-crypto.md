# Brief: Cape Crypto

## Business
Cape Crypto is a South African cryptocurrency exchange (Cape Town-based, trading since 2020), authorised FSP (No. 53746). Core product: buy/sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, USDT via ZAR bank transfer. Also offers Wealth Management, Merchant Services, Developer API.

## What They Need
Conversion-focused marketing site driving sign-ups to trade.capecrypto.com. Explain "buy crypto in a minute" simplicity, build trust (FSP-regulated, local), route users to app download or web trading.

## Key Requirements
- Hero: value prop + primary CTA (Sign up), South African flag/local trust cue
- 3-step "how it works" (Sign Up → Deposit → Buy Bitcoin) w/ app screenshots
- Feature grid: lowest fees, easy ZAR deposits, Lightning Network support
- Partner logos section (Numeral, Aluma, etc.)
- FAQ/support section linking to Help Centre
- Footer: Overview/Product/Support nav, legal links (Terms, Privacy, Travel Rule), FSP disclaimer, copyright
- App store badges (Google Play + App Store)
- Persistent Sign in / Sign up CTAs in header

## Target Audience
- South African retail investors new to crypto (simplicity emphasized)
- ZAR-holders wanting low-fee on/off ramp
- Merchants/businesses (separate Merchant Services page)
- Developers wanting API access

## Brand Personality
- Trustworthy, regulated, local-first ("Proudly South African")
- Simple, fast, no-friction ("nothing else required")
- Value-driven, price-competitive tone
- Clean fintech, not hype-y crypto-bro tone

## Specific Requests
- Preserve 3-step onboarding visual (numbered steps + screenshots)
- Keep FSP compliance disclaimer prominent in footer
- Keep dual app-store badges
- Partner/trust-logo strip

## Technical Notes
- Standard Next.js build, deploy Vercel
- trade.capecrypto.com (app) and support.capecrypto.com (Zendesk help centre) stay external — marketing site links out, no auth/trading logic in scope
- Fee schedule content sourced from support article — link, don't duplicate maintenance burden
