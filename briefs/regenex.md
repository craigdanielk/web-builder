# Brief: Regenex

## Business
- Company: Regenex
- Location: South Africa (international entity structure)
- Founded: 2025
- Focus: Medical e-commerce platform for hair regrowth and medical products
- Heritage: Founded by Larry Girchowitz, South Africa's top hair transplant surgeon, transitioning from clinical practice to direct-to-consumer medical products

## What They Need
- Professional medical e-commerce storefront
- Product catalogue with dosage, pack size, pricing
- SAHPRA-compliant presentation (no unsubstantiated medical claims)
- Consultation booking integration
- Blog/educational content section
- Mobile-responsive, trustworthy medical aesthetic

## Key Requirements
- Product categories: Supplements & OTC (Phase 1), Prescription Medications (Phase 2)
- Payment: PayFast + Yoco for RSA market
- Courier integration for RSA-based delivery
- Customer profiles and login
- Prescription upload capability (manual initially)
- Educational content positioning generics as quality alternatives

## Target Audience
- Primary: South African adults (25-65) experiencing hair loss
- Secondary: Patients seeking affordable generic medication alternatives
- Tertiary: Medical professionals referring patients
- Price positioning: Professional medical, competitive vs branded

## Brand Personality
- Colors: Clean medical white (#FFFFFF) primary, deep teal (#0D7377) accent, slate gray (#334155) text
- Typography: Clean, professional sans-serif (Inter or similar medical-grade typography)
- Imagery: Clinical but approachable, professional product photography, before/after results
- Tone: Evidence-based, trustworthy, educational, empathetic

## Product Details
- Product line: Hair regrowth supplements, topical treatments, medical shampoos
- Regulatory: SAHPRA-compliant labelling and advertising
- USP: Formulated by South Africa's leading hair transplant surgeon
- Pricing: Competitive with branded alternatives (generics positioning)
- Distribution: Direct-to-consumer online, RSA initially

## Specific Requests
- Hero: Medical credibility with surgeon endorsement, "Science-Backed Hair Regrowth"
- About: Dr. Larry Girchowitz's credentials and philosophy
- Products: Clean product grid with dosage information and pricing
- How It Works: Hair regrowth science explained simply
- Testimonials: Patient results with before/after (SAHPRA compliant)
- FAQ: Medical questions about hair loss treatment, generic vs branded
- Blog: Educational articles on hair health
- Contact: Consultation booking + contact form
- Trust: SAHPRA compliance badge, secure checkout, privacy policy

## Technical Notes
- Platform: Next.js headless storefront (Shopify Storefront API)
- Preset: health-wellness
- Products: 10-20 SKUs initially (supplements, topicals, shampoos)
- Markets: South Africa primary, international future
- Compliance: SAHPRA advertising and labelling compliance required
- Payments: PayFast, Yoco (RSA gateways)
