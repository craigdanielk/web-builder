# Brief: shopjoga

## Business
Joga is a performance soccer/football equipment brand specializing in innovative protective gear and performance accessories for players. They're known for pioneering mini shin pads and grip socks, with products endorsed by professional athletes. The brand serves a global market with a focus on next-generation equipment that enhances player performance and recovery. Their unique value proposition centers on revolutionary products like "the world's first mini shin pad" and pro-backed performance bundles, emphasizing innovation over traditional bulky equipment.

## What They Need
An e-commerce website that drives product sales through aggressive promotional campaigns, social proof from professional endorsements, and clear product education. The site must create urgency through limited-time offers while establishing credibility through athlete partnerships and customer testimonials. Primary conversion goals include pushing bestsellers, bundle sales, and showcasing their innovative product differentiation in a crowded sports equipment market.

## Key Requirements
- Prominent promotional banner system with countdown timers for flash sales (BOGO, percentage discounts)
- Warehouse Sale section highlighting clearance inventory
- Bestsellers showcase featuring 10+ hero products with product cards
- Performance Bundles section (Stamina Bundle, Baller's Sleep Bundle, Cold Bundle, Recovery Bundle)
- Product collections organized by category (Shin Pads, Socks, Accessories)
- "Hall of Fame" / athlete endorsement section showcasing professional players who use the products
- "Joga Starz" kids/youth product section
- Social proof elements including testimonials, statistics, and trust indicators
- Custom product builder functionality (evidenced by "Create your own Shin Pad")
- Product detail pages with compelling copy explaining innovation and benefits
- FAQ section addressing common customer questions
- Country/region and language selector for international customers
- Customer review/rating display system

## Target Audience
- Competitive soccer players (amateur to professional level) seeking performance advantages
- Youth players and parents looking for innovative, safe equipment
- Athletes recovering from injuries or managing chronic pain (evidenced by Recovery Sleeve)
- Performance-focused players willing to invest in premium equipment bundles
- Global market spanning Europe and other regions (multiple language/region support)
- Tech-savvy sports consumers who value innovation and professional endorsements

## Brand Personality
- Performance-driven and competitive ("Get ahead of the competition")
- Innovative and disruptive ("revolutionary," "next-gen," "world's first")
- Youth-oriented and energetic ("Built for the next gen," "Joga Starz")
- Credible and professional (heavy use of athlete endorsements)
- Bold and confident (declarative headlines, competitive positioning)
- Community-focused ("Built on respect, hard work, and the love of the beautiful game")
- Urgent and promotional (countdown timers, aggressive sale messaging)

## Specific Requests
- Maintain the layered promotional strategy with countdown timers creating urgency
- Preserve the athlete endorsement showcase as a key credibility builder
- Keep the bundle-focused merchandising strategy prominent
- Retain product education approach with benefit-driven copy rather than just features
- Continue emphasizing innovation and "world's first" messaging in product positioning
- Maintain separate kids/youth section (Joga Starz) with appropriate messaging
- Preserve the statistics/social proof section ("THE STATS BEHIND THE HYPE")
- Keep founder story section to humanize the brand
- Maintain global testimonial display ("TRUSTED BY BALLERS ACROSS THE GLOBE")

## Technical Notes
- E-commerce platform with robust promotional campaign management (percentage discounts, BOGO, countdown timers)
- Multi-currency and multi-language support for international sales
- Product customization functionality for custom shin pad builder
- Inventory management system supporting warehouse sale/clearance sections
- Dynamic bundle creation and pricing
- Integration with review/rating platform
- Fast-loading product imagery (multiple product shots per item)
- Mobile-optimized checkout flow given athletic/on-the-go audience
- Email capture for promotional campaigns
- Analytics tracking for conversion optimization on promotional campaigns
